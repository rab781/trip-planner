## 2024-06-11 - [Title: Accessible Collapsible Containers]
**Learning:** Custom interactive elements like custom collapsible components need robust accessibility, including aria-expanded on the toggle button, aria-controls pointing to the ID of the controlled content, and distinct focus styles for keyboard users.
**Action:** Always verify custom interactives have proper ARIA states, ID associations, and visual focus rings when creating or updating custom collapsibles.
